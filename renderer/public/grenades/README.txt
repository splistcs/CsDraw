Grenade icons used by the demo viewer (player inventories, items on the ground, grenades in flight and the kill feed)
and also available to the tactical board.

Expected files (transparent PNG, preferably square, ~128x128 or larger):

  he.png  flash.png  smoke.png  molotov.png  incendiary.png  decoy.png

While a file is missing, the app falls back to an emoji (board) or a colored vector icon (demo viewer).
Weapon and C4 icons live in ../weapons/.
